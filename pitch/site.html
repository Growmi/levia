Questions

What people ask first

Is it safe to fly indoors?

Levia flies only in empty zones, after hours. The propellers sit inside guarded ducts, and LiDAR plus obstacle sensors keep it clear of people and furniture. Most of the work happens on wheels; flight is for changing floors and reaching high places.

Doesn’t flying blow dust around?

Levia cleans an area first and flies afterwards, with slow, low landings. The HEPA filter also cleans the air while it flies.

How does it open and lock doors?

Door sensors report which rooms are open. The robotic arm handles standard doors, and locking only happens through your building’s access-control system, with admin approval.

What about privacy?

Images are processed on the robot, people are blurred, and building data stays in the building.

What happens to lost items?

The camera recognises keys, badges and phones. The arm picks them up, the app logs where they were found, and they wait at reception.
