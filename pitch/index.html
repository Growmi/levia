Teams to coordinate on three floors, and last-minute absences

One Levia covers every floor and starts by itself, every night.

It cleans each room as soon as the booking ends.

A report before 8:30, lost items at reception, alerts if a part needs care.

Technical challenges

Designed to be feasible

01
Battery lifeCleans mainly on wheels, flies only between floors. Target: 6–8 hours of mixed operation.
02
WeightCarries only a small amount of water and returns to its Hub to refill and recharge.
03
NoiseShort flights, mainly at night or when the building is less occupied.
04
SafetyProtected rotors and 360° sensors detect people and obstacles and stop the flight.
05
DustVacuums on the ground and flies only when needed.

Levia doesn’t fly to clean. It flies to reach the next cleaning area.
